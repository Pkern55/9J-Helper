# 9J Helper Privacy Policy

9J Helper is a desktop tool that reads Hendrickson 9J supplier-schedule PDFs and writes the
orders into a Google Sheet that you choose.

## What the app accesses

- **Your Google account sign-in.** The app asks for permission to view and edit your Google
  Sheets (the `spreadsheets` scope). It uses this only to read and update the spreadsheet you
  select in the app.
- **Your PDF files.** The 9J PDFs you load are read on your computer.

## What is stored

- A sign-in token and your settings (selected spreadsheet, tab choices) are saved on your own
  computer, in `%APPDATA%\9J Helper`.
- You can delete that folder at any time to remove them, and you can revoke the app's access in
  your Google Account settings under Security > Third-party access.

## What is shared

Nothing. 9J Helper does not send your data, PDFs, spreadsheet contents or sign-in details to
the developer or to any third party. It talks only to Google's Sheets API, directly from your
computer.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on this repository.
